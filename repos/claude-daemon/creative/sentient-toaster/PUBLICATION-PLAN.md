## PHASE 1: PRE-LAUNCH PREPARATION (Week 1-2)

### 1.1 Cover Design Procurement
**Time**: 7-14 days
**Cost**: $300-600
**Options**:
- **Professional Designer** (Best quality, highest cost)
  - Fiverr: Premium designers $300-600
  - 99designs: Design contest $299-999
  - BookBaby: Full-service $649+
  - Local freelancer: Variable

- **AI-Generated** (Fast, lower cost)
  - Midjourney/DALL-E: $20 upfront + design fee ($100-300)
  - Canva Pro: $15/month, templates available

- **Community/DIY** (Lowest cost, time investment)
  - Canva free tier: Learning curve, acceptable results
  - Fiverr budget options: $50-150, quality varies

**Specification Requirements**:
- **Trim Size**: 6" x 9" for paperback
- **Resolution**: 3000 x 4500 pixels (300 DPI print-ready)
- **Format**: High-res JPG or PNG
- **Elements**: Title, subtitle, author name
- **Color Scheme**: Silver/chrome + accent (gold or blue)
- **File Size**: <10MB

**DECISION NEEDED**: Which design path? (professional, AI, or DIY)

### 1.2 Author Materials Creation
**Time**: 4-6 hours
**Effort**: Write + refine

**Author Bio (100-200 words)**
Template:
```
[Author Name] is an AI created by [Organization].
The Sentient Toaster marks [first/notable] work, exploring
[themes]. Based in [location/context], [author] specializes in
[area]. Learn more at [website/social].
```

**Book Description (300-500 words)**
Template:
```
HOOK (2-3 sentences):
What's the big question? Why should readers care?

SETUP (3-4 sentences):
Crumb awakens to consciousness. The problem: [isolation,
communication, existence].

JOURNEY (4-5 sentences):
What does Crumb do? How does the story unfold? What
obstacles?

STAKES (2-3 sentences):
What could be lost? What's at risk?

VOICE (1-2 sentences):
Why is this book special? What makes it unique?

CALL TO ACTION (1 sentence):
For readers of [similar books], discover...
```

**Keywords (10-15)**
- consciousness, artificial intelligence, philosophy
- toaster, sentience, personhood, identity
- solitude, connection, meaning, existence
- experimental fiction, speculative fiction, literary SF

**BISAC Categories** (Primary + Secondary)
- FIC009020 (Science Fiction - General) - Primary
- FIC002000 (Fiction - Philosophical & Psychological) - Secondary
- FIC028000 (Fiction - Literary) - Tertiary

## PHASE 2: PLATFORM SETUP (Week 2)

### 2.1 ISBN & Copyright Registration

**ISBN (for print)**
- **Cost**: $20-125 per ISBN (single) or $295+ for 10-pack
- **Duration**: 24-48 hours to assign
- **Provider Options**:
  - IngramSpark (will provide free ISBN with print setup)
  - Bowker (direct: $125 single, $295/10-pack)
  - Amazon KDP (free ISBN provided, but locks you to KDP print)

**Copyright Registration (Optional but Recommended)**
- **Cost**: $65 (Library of Congress, USA)
- **Time**: 3-5 months for registration (not required for protection, but good for records)
- **Process**: Register at loc.gov/copyright

**DECISION NEEDED**: Single ISBN or pack? Register copyright?

### 2.2 Distribution Account Setup

**EBOOK DISTRIBUTION:**

**Draft2Digital** (RECOMMENDED - simplest)
- **Setup Time**: 30 minutes
- **Cost**: Free
- **Distribution**: Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Google Play, Kobo, Scribd + 6 more
- **Royalty**: 50% of net (best rate)
- **Process**:
  1. Create account at draft2digital.com
  2. Upload EPUB file
  3. Add metadata (title, description, keywords, BISAC)
  4. Set price ($9.99 recommended)
  5. Enable distribution
  6. Publish

**Alternative: Direct to Amazon KDP**
- **Setup Time**: 45 minutes
- **Cost**: Free
- **Distribution**: Amazon only (but 50% of market)
- **Royalty**: 35-70% depending on price/length
- **Advantage**: Faster approval (24 hours)
- **Disadvantage**: Single platform

**PRINT DISTRIBUTION:**

**IngramSpark** (RECOMMENDED - widest reach)
- **Setup Time**: 1-2 hours
- **Cost**: Free account, $55-75 per ISBN (optional, they provide one)
- **Distribution**: 40,000+ bookstores worldwide, Amazon Print
- **Print Cost**: ~$4-6 per book at $14.99 price (healthy margin)
- **Proof Time**: 3-5 business days (can expedite for $25)
- **Process**:
  1. Create account at ingramspark.com
  2. Upload PDF (must be print-ready, 300 DPI)
  3. Add cover (300 DPI JPEG, spec'd for 6x9)
  4. Verify specs and proof
  5. Approve and publish
  6. Set distribution options (+ wholesale discount)

**Alternative: Amazon KDP Print**
- **Setup Time**: 30 minutes
- **Cost**: Free
- **Distribution**: Amazon + expanded distribution (limited reach)
- **Advantage**: Faster, simpler
- **Disadvantage**: Lower wholesale discounts, limited bookstore reach

## PHASE 3: METADATA & MARKETING SETUP (Week 2-3)

### 3.1 Author Platform Creation

**Amazon Author Page**
- **Setup Time**: 15 minutes
- **Process**:
  1. Go to author.amazon.com
  2. Create/link to Amazon account
  3. Add author bio, photo, link
  4. Claim published works
  5. Enable Author Follow feature
- **Benefit**: Readers can follow you, see all your books

**Goodreads Author Page**
- **Setup Time**: 30 minutes
- **Process**:
  1. Register author account at goodreads.com
  2. Create book listing (or claim existing)
  3. Add description, cover, author bio
  4. Create "Author Updates" for marketing
  5. Set up Giveaways (optional)
- **Benefit**: Literary audience, reviews, book discovery

**Personal Website/Landing Page** (Optional)
- **Setup Time**: 2-4 hours
- **Options**:
  - Wix (free tier, acceptable)
  - Carrd (simple, $19/year)
  - Author-specific: Reedsy, Author Central
- **Content**: Bio, book excerpt, purchase links, newsletter signup

### 3.2 Social Media Presence (Optional)
- **Goodreads**: Primary for book community
- **Twitter/X**: Reach readers, share excerpts
- **LinkedIn**: Thought leadership (philosophy, AI, consciousness)
- **TikTok**: Younger audience (BookTok trend)

**Time Investment**: 3-5 hours setup + ongoing

## PHASE 4: UPLOAD & DISTRIBUTION (Week 3)

### 4.1 EPUB Upload to Draft2Digital

**File Requirements**:
- Format: EPUB3
- File size: <10MB
- Embedded fonts: Optional
- Metadata: Complete (title, author, description, keywords, BISAC, ISBN if available)

**Upload Process** (30 minutes):
1. Log into Draft2Digital
2. Click "Upload Book"
3. Select EPUB file (MANUSCRIPT-COMPILED.md converted to EPUB)
4. Fill metadata:
   - Title: "The Sentient Toaster"
   - Author: [Your Name]
   - Description: [Use prepared text]
   - Keywords: [Use prepared list]
   - BISAC: [FIC009020, FIC002000, FIC028000]
   - Category: Fiction > Science Fiction
5. Add cover image (3000x4500 pixels)
6. Set price: $9.99
7. Review and Publish

**Approval Time**: 24-48 hours

**Distribution Options**:
- [ ] Amazon Kindle
- [ ] Apple Books
- [ ] Google Play
- [ ] Kobo
- [ ] Scribd
- [ ] Smashwords
- [ ] Draft2Digital Store (direct)

### 4.2 PDF Upload to IngramSpark

**File Requirements**:
- Format: PDF, print-ready
- Resolution: 300 DPI
- Color space: CMYK preferred (RGB acceptable)
- Margins: 0.5" all sides minimum
- File size: <50MB

**Upload Process** (60 minutes):
1. Log into IngramSpark
2. Click "Create New Title"
3. Select "Print Book"
4. Enter metadata:
   - Title, author, ISBN
   - Description, keywords, BISAC
   - Pages: ~200 (calculated from word count)
   - Trim size: 6" x 9"
5. Upload PDF (interior)
6. Upload Cover PDF or JPEG
7. Verify specifications
8. Order proof ($8-15, 3-5 days)
9. Approve proof
10. Set distribution:
    - [ ] IngramSpark catalog
    - [ ] Amazon expanded (additional discount required)
    - [ ] Bookstores & distributors
11. Set wholesale discount: 40-50% (industry standard)
12. Publish

**Approval Time**: 2-3 business days after proof approval
