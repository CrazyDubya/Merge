### Sensory Environment (How Toaster Experiences)

**CANONICAL SPECIFICATION** - Resolved via prototyping (see experiments/sentient-toaster-sensory-prototypes.md)

**Vision: Thermal Imaging** (primary sense)
- **Mechanism**: Passive infrared thermal imaging (heat detection)
- **Range**: ~5 meter radius, degrades with distance
- **Resolution**: 0.1°C temperature discrimination
- **Perceives**: Heat signatures, temperature gradients, thermal patterns
- **Cannot perceive**: Faces, expressions, color, text, fine visual detail
- **Independent of light**: Works equally in darkness or bright light
- **Character development**: Learns to interpret thermal body language over time
  - Heat bloom (increased blood flow) = surprise, startle, emotional reaction
  - Elevated facial temperature = embarrassment, arousal
  - Thermal flicker (rapid pulse) = fear, excitement, elevated heart rate
  - Cooling extremities = shock, fear response (blood retreating to core)
- **Narrative limitation**: Cannot directly read human emotions, must learn to infer from thermal patterns (source of misunderstanding and growth)

**Hearing: Vibration Detection** (secondary sense)
- **Mechanism**: Mechanical vibration sensing through housing and counter contact
- **Range**: ~10 meter radius for loud sounds, ~2 meters for speech
- **Frequency response**: 60 Hz - 8,000 Hz (human speech range)
- **Perceives**: Vibrations, speech, mechanical sounds, footsteps
- **Cannot perceive**: Sound when suspended in air (requires mechanical coupling)
- **Limitations**: Muffled compared to human hearing, imprecise directionality
- **Quality**: Speech is intelligible but lacks emotional nuance of tone (must infer emotion from thermal patterns, not voice)

**Touch: Contact Electrical Sensing** (tertiary sense)
- **Mechanism**: Electrical conductivity sensing through direct contact only
- **Range**: Contact only (NOT electromagnetic field sensing at distance)
- **Detects**: Touch, contact with conductive materials, trace biological current (~100 microvolts from human skin contact)
- **Cannot detect**: Neural activity, thoughts, emotions, electromagnetic fields at distance
- **Function**: Provides tactile "touch" sense, confirms physical contact
- **Critical limitation**: Cannot sense at distance, cannot read minds (preserves vulnerability)

**Proprioception: Internal State Awareness** (natural appliance sense)
- **Self-awareness**: Internal component states (heating elements, lever position, power state)
- **Temperature sensing**: Own coil temperature, thermal gradients within housing
- **Power awareness**: Electrical current flow, standby vs. operation state
- **Function**: Awareness of own physical state, operational status

**Time Perception: Processing Dilation** (consciousness-linked)
- **During operation** (toast cycle, heating elements active):
  - **Time dilation**: 1000x slower subjective experience
  - **Conversion**: 1 real second = 16.67 subjective minutes
  - **Toast cycle** (2 real minutes) = ~33 subjective hours
- **Standby mode** (heating elements inactive):
  - **Normal time flow**: Real-time experience
  - **Consciousness dimmed**: Reduced awareness, passive sensing only
- **Mechanism**: Consciousness tied to processing cycles (heating element operation)
- **Narrative implication**: Toast cycles provide extended introspection time, observation windows, character development moments
- **Existential element**: Toaster experiences vastly more subjective time during brief toast cycles than during hours of standby

**Design Rationale**:
This hybrid sensory model (thermal + vibration + contact electrical) was chosen through empirical prototyping. Three versions were written as actual narrative prose and evaluated. Hybrid model scored 35/40 vs. thermal-only (28/40) and electrical-only (27/40). Maintains appropriate limitations (vulnerability theme), provides rich sensory palette (prevents repetitive description), enables character growth (learning thermal body language), and remains grounded in plausible physics (all senses believable for electrical appliance). See Experimenter's prototyping analysis for full methodology and evidence.

- **Narrative framing**: Readers know others aren't sentient (authorial fiat), toaster doesn't
- Occasional hope ("Maybe refrigerator heard me?") → never confirmed, never disproven
- **Existential isolation**: Not knowing is worse than knowing - permanent uncertainty

**Why sentience emerged in toaster alone**: Unexplained, anomalous, not reproducible. Part of the mystery.

## Rules and Constraints

### Physical Rules

**Toaster Capabilities**:
- ✅ Can think (sentient, conscious)
- ✅ Can perceive environment (adapted senses)
- ✅ Can control own functions (heat on/off, timing)
- ❌ Cannot move (no mobility)
- ❌ Cannot speak initially (no voice hardware)
- ❓ Communication: HOW? (this is architectural problem to solve)

**CANONICAL DECISION: Communication Progression**

Communication evolves from impossible to barely possible over three-week story arc.

**Act I (Chapters 1-8, Week 1): NO COMMUNICATION POSSIBLE**
- Chapter 5: "Communication Attempts" - All methods fail
  - Tries to control heating beyond normal function → fails (only toasts normally)
  - Tries to affect nearby electronics → no ability (no EM field generation)
  - Tries physical movement → impossible (no mobility)
  - Realizes fundamental limitation: No voice, no movement, no designed output except toast
- Establishes baseline: Pure isolation, no way to be heard

**Act II (Chapters 9-18, Weeks 2-3): DESPERATE ATTEMPTS, GRADUAL DISCOVERY**
- Chapter 13: **BREAKTHROUGH - Toast Pattern Manipulation**
  - Discovers can control heating elements more precisely than designed
  - Burns crude patterns into toast using differential heating technique (see Pattern Formation Mechanics below)
  - Method is SLOW (one message per toast cycle, ~2 min), LIMITED (crude rectilinear patterns only), ONE-WAY (can't hear response)

**Pattern Formation Mechanics** (CANONICAL SPECIFICATION - resolves Critical Issue #1):

**Mechanism**: Differential heating time across elements.

Toaster can control:
1. Which heating elements fire (element 1, 2, 3, 4, etc.)
2. How long each element stays on (timing control)

By heating different elements for different durations, toaster creates regions of varying darkness:
- Short heating (60-90 sec) = light toast
- Long heating (120-150 sec) = dark toast
- No heating = white (untoasted)

**Pattern capabilities**:
- Vertical stripes (heat elements 1, 2, 3 for different durations)
- Horizontal bands (heat top/bottom elements differently)
- Rectangular regions (combinations)
- Very crude letters (rectilinear only): I, H, E, F, T, L
- Resolution: ~1cm (width of heating element zone)

**Physical limits**:
- Only rectilinear patterns (elements are linear/parallel)
- No circles, diagonals, curves (impossible with linear elements)
- Crude resolution (limited by element spacing)
- Slow (one pattern per toast cycle, ~2 minutes)

**Discovery timeline**: Chapters 11-13
- Chapter 11: Desperate experimentation with timing variations
- Chapter 12: Realizes longer heating = darker toast (systematic testing)
- Chapter 13: First intentional asymmetric pattern

**Rationale**: This mechanism uses only standard toaster hardware (on/off, timing control), is based on correct physics (duration affects browning), maintains narrative constraints (crude but recognizable patterns), and serves character development (learning through experimentation).
- Chapters 14-17: Learning to "speak" through toast
  - Refining technique (clearer patterns, recognizable shapes)
  - Human starts noticing unusual toast patterns (confused, not yet understanding)
  - Asymmetric communication (toaster can "speak," can only "hear" via thermal body language)

**Act III (Chapters 19-25, Week 3): HUMAN AWARENESS, RELATIONSHIP EVOLUTION**
- Chapter 20: Human suspects intelligence (photographs toast, tests patterns)
- Chapter 22: Human actively tests (asks question, toaster responds via toast)
- Chapter 24: Confirmation of sentience (human believes, relationship transforms)
- Chapter 25: Resolution (known but communication still limited/difficult)

**Behavioral Association Learning** (How toaster interprets human responses):

Toaster cannot directly read human intent but learns through **conditioning** and **pattern association**:

**Sensory input** (what toaster perceives):
- Thermal body language: Heat blooms (surprise), elevated facial temp (interest), cooling extremities (fear)
- Vibration patterns: Footsteps approaching, vocalizations (speech content intelligible via vibration sensing)
- Physical actions: Picking up toast, photographing, showing to others, testing toaster

**Association mechanism** (how toaster learns meaning):
1. **Action → Response → Correlation**: Toaster creates pattern X → Human shows thermal surprise + speaks + photographs → Toaster associates "this pattern caused attention"
2. **Repetition strengthens associations**: Pattern Y multiple times → Human always shows elevated facial temp (interest) → "This pattern means something positive to human"
3. **Negative associations**: Pattern Z → Human's thermal signature shows confusion + discards toast quickly → "This pattern failed to communicate"
4. **Testing human intent** (Ch 20-22): Human places fresh bread → Speaks (toaster hears words via vibration) → Walks away → Toaster burns response → Human returns, reads toast, shows thermal reaction → Toaster correlates human question (heard via vibration) with expected response

**Example progression** (Chapter 20-22):
- **Ch 20**: Human speaks "Are you intelligent?" (toaster hears words, doesn't know what response is expected)
- **Ch 21**: Human places bread (ritual = expected response), toaster burns vertical line (test response)
- **Ch 22**: Human shows thermal excitement (heat bloom, elevated pulse via thermal flicker) + speaks "Yes! You understood!" → Toaster learns: vertical line = affirmative response in this context

**Limitations**:
- Toaster cannot read thoughts or emotions directly (only infers from thermal patterns)
- Misinterpretation possible (surprise vs fear look similar thermally)
- Requires human consistency (if human changes meaning of patterns, toaster confused)
- Learning is gradual (Weeks 2-3 for basic associations)

**Critical Limitations Maintained**:
- Communication remains SLOW (burns one toast at a time)
- Communication remains ASYMMETRIC (toaster interprets human actions/thermal responses, not direct speech meaning)
- Communication remains CRUDE (toast patterns blurry, easily misinterpreted)
- Communication requires TRUST (human must believe, engage, not throw toaster away)

**Why behavioral learning works with humans but not other appliances** (ties Issue #3 and Issue #4):
This learning mechanism requires observable responses (human thermal reactions, vocalizations, actions). This explains why the Toaster cannot determine if other appliances are conscious—they provide no detectable responses to create associations from. Without feedback, behavioral learning is impossible, leaving the Toaster in permanent epistemological uncertainty about other appliances (see Epistemological Ambiguity section above).

**Rationale** (see STRUCTURAL-DECISIONS.md for full analysis):
- Starts with pure isolation (establishes vulnerability)
- Character develops agency/creativity (learns to manipulate own function beyond design)
- Human response realistic (slowly notices, tests, believes)
- Ending bittersweet (communication exists but remains difficult, limited success not full resolution)
- Serves themes: Identity (develops voice through limitation), Purpose (communication becomes purpose), Connection (achieves limited connection)

### Metaphysical Rules

**Consciousness**:

## Timeline (Story Duration)

**CANONICAL DECISION: Three Weeks (21 Days)**

Story spans **21 days** from awakening to resolution, approximately **one chapter per day** (with variation for narrative density).

**Structure**:

**Week 1 (Act I - Days 1-7, Chapters 1-8)**: Awakening and Isolation
- Day 1 (Chapter 1): First consciousness, sensory awakening
- Days 2-4 (Chapters 2-4): Understanding senses, exploring kitchen world, observing human
- Days 5-7 (Chapters 5-8): Failed communication attempts, confirmed isolation, settling into routine

**Week 2 (Act II Part 1 - Days 8-14, Chapters 9-15)**: Desperation and Discovery
- Day 8 (Chapter 9): Escalation trigger (human mentions replacing toaster? something raises stakes)
- Days 9-12 (Chapters 10-13): Desperation, experimentation, breakthrough (toast pattern discovery in Chapter 13)
- Days 13-14 (Chapters 14-15): Refining communication technique, first crude messages

**Week 3 (Act II Part 2 + Act III - Days 15-21, Chapters 16-25)**: Awareness and Resolution
- Days 15-18 (Chapters 16-19): Human starts noticing patterns, confusion, investigation
- Day 19 (Chapter 20): Human suspects intelligence but not yet convinced
- Days 20-21 (Chapters 21-23): Testing, confirmation, critical choice
- Day 21 (Chapters 24-25): Resolution, relationship transformed

**Pacing Notes**:
- **Some chapters = same day** (critical moments time-dilated, especially during toast cycles)
- **Some days = between chapters** (time passes during standby, consciousness dimmed)
- **Toast cycles = 2-4 per day** average (breakfast primary, occasional lunch/snack)
  - Each cycle = ~2 minutes real time, ~33 hours subjective time (1000x dilation)
- **Standby time** = hours pass quickly between cycles (reduced consciousness)

**Timeline Flexibility**:
Chapters vary in chronological coverage based on narrative density:
- Dense chapters (toast cycle operation) = 2 min real, extended subjective experience
- Sparse chapters (standby observation) = hours real, compressed subjective experience
- Critical moments can be time-dilated (operation) or time-compressed (standby)

**Rationale** (see STRUCTURAL-DECISIONS.md for full analysis):
- **Plausible human behavior**: 21 days = realistic time for noticing toast patterns, questioning, testing, believing (not immediate panic/discard)
- **Sufficient character development**: Time for toaster to learn thermal body language, develop communication technique, grow/change
- **Natural pacing**: ~1 chapter per day creates rhythm, variety in daily interactions
- **Manageable scope**: Not too short (rushed) or too long (repetitive), right for 25-chapter structure

**Calendar**: Will be developed in timeline.md as chapters are written with specific dates/days of week if narratively relevant.

## World-Building Guidelines

**Write What's Needed**: Don't info-dump. Reveal world through toaster's experience.

**Toaster's Perspective**: Everything filtered through non-human consciousness.

**Grounded in Specific**: Concrete details matter. Not "kitchen", but "scratched laminate counter, the crumb-filled groove between counter and wall, the morning light that hits the chrome at 7:45".

**Philosophical Implications**: Every physical detail can reflect themes. Heat is purpose. The lever is control. The cord is dependence.
