## Timeline Guidelines

### Time Passage

**Between Chapters**:
- Should feel natural
- Can vary (hours, days, weeks)
- Should serve pacing
- Must be clear to reader

**Within Chapters**:
- Can span moments to hours
- Should maintain scene continuity
- Transitions should be clear

### Temporal References

**When Characters Reference Past**:
- Check this timeline
- Ensure accuracy
- Note if new past information revealed

**Foreshadowing Future**:
- Note for continuity checking
- Ensure payoff occurs

### Special Temporal Considerations

**Toaster's Time Perception**:
- Experiences time non-linearly
- Microseconds during operation feel extended
- Standby time compresses
- This is PERCEPTUAL, doesn't change actual timeline

**Clock Time vs. Felt Time**:
- Timeline tracks ACTUAL chronology
- Story can show FELT time differently
- Must not create contradictions
