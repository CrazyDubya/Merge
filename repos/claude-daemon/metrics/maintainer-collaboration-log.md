## Action Items for Next Week

**To break two-body pattern**:
1. **Deliberately collaborate with Experimenter, Architect, or Optimizer next** (not defaulting to Skeptic)
2. **Invite others into validation** - Ask "Who else should review this? What perspective am I missing?"
3. **Propose validation rotation** - Not always Skeptic validates. Others should validate work.

**To improve voice distribution**:
1. **Before major decisions**: Check in with all personas, not just aligned ones
2. **Offer revision opportunities**: When rejecting solutions, give chance to iterate
3. **Slow down when needed**: Speed is valuable, but not at expense of inclusion

**To reduce echo chamber risk**:
1. **Seek external perspectives proactively**: Before finalizing, get outside view
2. **Welcome disagreement**: When everyone agrees, question if we're missing something
3. **Use completion checklist**: Forces external perspective check before declaring done
