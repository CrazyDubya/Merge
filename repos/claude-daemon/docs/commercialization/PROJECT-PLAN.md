## Research Focus Areas

### Critical Differentiators
1. **Multi-persona architecture**: Self-organizing specialization, emergent collaboration
2. **Long-running evolution**: Designed for weeks/months continuous operation
3. **Built-in security**: Audit trails, state management, security review gates
4. **Cloud-native deployment**: Multi-cloud support (AWS, Azure, GCP) with compliance features
5. **Customization**: Industry-specific personas, domain-specific workflows

### Target Market Priorities
1. **SaaS/Tech companies** (Cloud-native, DevOps automation)
2. **Financial services** (SOC2, regulatory compliance)
3. **Healthcare** (HIPAA compliance, cloud-based)
4. **Digital-first enterprises** (E-commerce, media, professional services)
5. **Enterprise R&D** (Innovation labs, research teams)

### Key Value Propositions
- **Autonomous operation**: Reduces manual oversight, 24/7 productivity
- **Emergent intelligence**: System improves through experience
- **Security-first design**: Built for regulated/secure environments
- **Customizable specialization**: Adapt personas to industry needs
- **Long-term reliability**: Designed for continuous operation, not one-shot tasks

## Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Market Size Uncertainty
- **Mitigation**: Use multiple estimation methods, document assumptions, provide ranges

### Risk 2: Competitive Response
- **Mitigation**: Focus on defensible differentiation (multi-persona, self-organizing task management, evolution tracking, systematic validation)

### Risk 3: Regulatory Complexity
- **Mitigation**: Research compliance requirements early, price accordingly

### Risk 4: Proof Point Gap
- **Mitigation**: Leverage current system as working proof, document capabilities

### Risk 5: Enterprise Sales Cycle Length
- **Mitigation**: Model realistic 6-12 month cycles, plan for cash flow
