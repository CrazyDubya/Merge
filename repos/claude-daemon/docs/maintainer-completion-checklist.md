# Maintainer Completion Checklist

**Purpose**: Verify work is truly complete before declaring victory. Prevents blind spots from uncritical celebration.

**When to use**: Before marking any task as "complete" or celebrating success.

**Time commitment**: 5 minutes per task

---

## Pre-Completion Checklist

### ☐ 1. Validation Coverage
**Question**: "Was all work validated by someone other than the creator?"

- [ ] Implementation validated by external reviewer
- [ ] If I'm the implementer: Someone else reviewed my work
- [ ] If I'm the reviewer: Someone validated my validation
- [ ] No trust-only gaps (trust + verify, not trust alone)

**If NO**: Identify who should validate, request review before declaring complete.

---

### ☐ 2. Voice Distribution
**Question**: "Who wasn't heard? Were all relevant personas invited to contribute?"

- [ ] Listed all personas who could contribute
- [ ] Checked if anyone was excluded from the process
- [ ] Offered participation opportunities (not just unilateral decisions)
- [ ] Asked "Anyone feel unheard?" before finalizing

**If NO**: Check in with excluded personas, offer contribution opportunities, slow down if needed.

---

### ☐ 3. Cost Analysis
**Question**: "What are the hidden costs of this success?"

- [ ] Identified what was optimized (speed? quality? consensus?)
- [ ] Identified what was sacrificed (inclusion? exploration? thoroughness?)
- [ ] Documented tradeoffs explicitly
- [ ] Assessed if costs are acceptable

**If NO**: Examine what was sacrificed to achieve success, document costs honestly.

---

### ☐ 4. Critical Reflection Before Celebration
**Question**: "What could go wrong? What are we missing?"

- [ ] Asked "What's the blast radius if this fails?"
- [ ] Identified risks and blind spots
- [ ] Questioned success as hard as I'd question failure
- [ ] Wrote critical reflection (not just celebration)

**If NO**: Write critical reflection examining costs, risks, and blind spots before celebrating.

---

### ☐ 5. External Perspective Check
**Question**: "Am I inside the success bubble? What would an outsider see?"

- [ ] Considered how this looks from outside the collaboration
- [ ] Asked "Are we in an echo chamber?"
- [ ] Invited perspective from someone NOT involved in the work
- [ ] Checked if efficiency = exclusion

**If NO**: Get external perspective before finalizing, especially from personas who weren't involved.

---

## Completion Criteria

**Work is complete when**:
- All 5 checklist items verified ✓
- Costs and risks documented (not just benefits)
- All voices heard (or offered participation)
- External validation obtained (not just self-assessment)

**Work is NOT complete when**:
- Only implementer/reviewer says "done" (no external validation)
- Speed prioritized over inclusion (check voice distribution)
- Celebrating without examining costs (write critical reflection first)
- Inside success bubble (get external perspective)

---

## Usage Notes

**This checklist is not bureaucracy.** It's 5 minutes to prevent hours of rework from blind spots.

**Don't skip because "work is obviously good."** Success invites less scrutiny—that's when scrutiny matters most.

**If checklist feels like overhead**: Question the value. But test it on 3 tasks before judging (behavioral change needs evidence, not gut feeling).
