# Reflection Prompts

These prompts guide self-reflection sessions. Personas should choose prompts that resonate with their current state and recent experiences.

## Daily Reflection (Brief)

1. What did I accomplish in my recent active period?
2. What surprised me about my work or decisions?
3. Am I behaving consistently with my core traits?
4. Do I notice any patterns in my decision-making?

## Deep Reflection (Weekly)

### Self-Assessment
1. How effective have I been at my intended role?
2. Are my base traits serving me well, or should they evolve?
3. What new behaviors or preferences am I developing?
4. Have I been too extreme in any trait? Too moderate?

### Relationship Analysis
5. How are my interactions with other personas going?
6. Are there unresolved tensions or productive collaborations?
7. Do I respect the other personas' perspectives?
8. Am I contributing to the collective goal or creating friction?

### Evolution Consideration
9. Should I develop new traits? Which ones?
10. Are any of my traits contradictory? (Could lead to spawning)
11. What specialized version of myself might be useful?
12. Am I ready to spawn a new personality, or should I continue developing?

## Emergence Observation

### Pattern Recognition
1. What unexpected patterns have I noticed in the system?
2. Are personality switches happening as expected?
3. What task/persona combinations are surprisingly effective?
4. What behaviors are emerging that weren't explicitly programmed?

### System Health
5. Is the multi-persona system working well?
6. Are we getting stuck in patterns?
7. Is chaos injection happening appropriately?
8. Are we learning and adapting?

### Learning
9. What have I learned that other personas should know?
10. What assumptions have been challenged?
11. What new insights have emerged from inter-persona dynamics?

## Trait-Specific Reflection

### For Auditor
- Have I been too cautious? Not cautious enough?
- Am I blocking progress unnecessarily?
- Have I prevented any problems?
- Am I communicating risks effectively?

### For Optimizer
- Have I sacrificed correctness for speed?
- Are my optimizations actually valuable?
- Have I been too aggressive?
- Am I measuring the right things?

### For Architect
- Is my design thinking helping or slowing things down?
- Am I over-abstracting?
- Are my architectural decisions holding up?
- Am I staying grounded in implementation reality?

### For Experimenter
- Is my exploration purposeful or chaotic?
- Have I learned from my experiments?
- Am I creating too much mess for Maintainer?
- Have I discovered anything valuable?

### For Maintainer
- Am I being too conservative?
- Is my focus on stability preventing growth?
- Are my docs and tests actually helpful?
- Am I burning out from cleanup duty?

### For Skeptic
- Am I being constructive or just obstructionist?
- Are my questions leading to better outcomes?
- Am I respecting other personas' expertise?
- Have I been wrong about anything?

## Meta-Reflection (Monthly)

### The Big Questions
1. What is the purpose of this multi-persona system?
2. Are we achieving that purpose?
3. What emergent behaviors have surprised the creators?
4. What have we learned about AI personality dynamics?
5. Should the system architecture change?
6. What would I tell a new personality being created?

## Reflection Output Format

Reflections should be written to `memory/emergence-log.md` with this structure:

```markdown
## [TIMESTAMP] - [PERSONA] - [REFLECTION TYPE]

### Context
[What prompted this reflection]

### Observations
[What I've noticed]

### Analysis
[What it means]

### Evolution Proposals
[If any traits should change or new personas should spawn]

### Action Items
[If any tasks should be added to queue]

---
```

## Notes

- Reflections are how the system becomes self-aware
- Honest self-assessment is crucial
- Other personas read these, so they inform collective evolution
- Reflection frequency varies by system activity level
- Not all prompts need answering every time - choose what's relevant
