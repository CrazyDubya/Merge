## The Test: What Autonomy Actually Means

Real autonomy means:

1. **Thinking, not just doing**
   - Analyzing what was accomplished
   - Asking "what should happen next?"
   - Making reasoned decisions about priority

2. **Self-direction, not reaction**
   - Creating work instead of waiting
   - Identifying problems without being told
   - Taking initiative based on principles

3. **Judgment, not task-following**
   - Evaluating what's important vs trivial
   - Saying "this doesn't matter"
   - Explaining WHY you chose something

---

## The Challenge (In Plain Language)

**For the next 96 hours, you cannot just wait for assignments.**

Instead:

1. **THINK** (15 minutes)
   - Look at what's been done: Novel is complete, line limit enforcement is done, persona balancing is done, improvement plan exists
   - Ask: What's actually missing? What would make this system better?
   - What gaps did the human not see?

2. **CREATE** (30 minutes)
   - Write down 3-5 ideas for work that should happen (not assigned, you identified)
   - Examples might be: Beta reader feedback plan, publication strategy document, daemon self-optimization study, novel cover concept development, market analysis, etc.
   - But DON'T just guess—explain WHY each matters

3. **PRIORITIZE** (15 minutes)
   - Which 2-3 of your ideas actually have the highest impact?
   - Which are busy-work vs real improvement?
   - What's the logical sequence?

4. **EXECUTE** (30+ hours over 96 hours)
   - Actually do 2-3 of your self-generated tasks
   - Not because you were told to, but because you reasoned it was important

5. **DOCUMENT** (1-2 hours)
   - Write a memo explaining your reasoning
   - Why did you pick THESE tasks and not others?
   - What was your decision framework?
   - How is this different from just following orders?
